CHURN PREDICTION AND THE RENEWAL OFFER IT DRIVES
================================================
lifecycle/ case 01. Carried across five files rather than one, because
the modelling section and the measurement section each deserve to be
read on their own.

Read context.md first. This case is written to that spec: the same
questions in the same order, and the answer written as speech.


THE ONE INSIGHT
---------------
The deliverable is not a churn score. It is a TREAT / DO-NOT-TREAT
policy under a margin budget, and the quantity that policy needs is not
P(lapse). It is tau - the change in renewal probability CAUSED by the
offer.

A churn model ranks by risk, so it spends the budget on everyone about
to leave: the people who will leave regardless, and the people who would
have renewed anyway. An uplift model ranks by incremental effect, so it
spends only on the people whose decision the offer actually changes.

The sentence the case turns on:

    not "who will churn?"
    but "whose renewal decision does an offer change?"

The second sentence is answerable only from randomised data. That is why
the randomisation, not the model, is the asset this project builds.


THE FOUR SEGMENTS THE POLICY HAS TO SEPARATE
--------------------------------------------
  SEGMENT         NO OFFER  WITH OFFER  ACTION
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
  Sure Things     renews    renews      skip - the discount is pure
                                        margin burn
  Persuadables    lapses    renews      TREAT - the only positive
                                        uplift in the table
  Lost Causes     lapses    lapses      skip - the spend is wasted
  Sleeping Dogs   renews    lapses      skip - the contact itself
                                        causes the loss

Sleeping Dogs are the segment nobody mentions unprompted and they are
real here. A renewal reminder makes a passive auto-renew charge into an
active decision: it reminds people they are paying, and it anchors them
on a price. They renew WORSE when treated. A propensity model cannot
see this segment at all, because it has no concept of a treatment, and
you can only detect it with a randomised holdout where the treated
group underperforms control.

WHERE THIS SITS ON THE SPINE
----------------------------
context.md section 4, mapped onto this case:

  SPINE STEP            WHERE IT IS ANSWERED HERE
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
  1 What are we paid    01_problem - expected revenue per subscriber,
    in?                 not renewal rate. The two conflict.
  2 What is the label?  02_label - renewed in [T, T+3], at
                        (user, cycle) grain, snapshot frozen at T-7.
                        Plus the assignment column, which is not a
                        label but decides whether the label is usable.
  3 How is it scored?   05_measurement - Qini offline, incremental
                        revenue per discount rupee online.
  4 What shape is it?   04_system - daily batch, no real-time
                        inference, because the renewal date is known
                        in advance.
  5 What goes in?       02_label - point-in-time features at T-7,
                        including past-discount and past-offer
                        exposure.
  6 What model?         03_modelling - calibrated GBM baseline, then
                        T-/X-learner uplift, then a dose-response
                        curve over a discrete depth grid.
  7 How does it ship?   04_system - explore, learn, exploit; the
                        holdout never ends.
  8 How does it rot?    04_system - discount anchoring, offer fatigue,
                        and the self-defeating prophecy.


SCOPE BOUNDARY
--------------
Subscription renewal only. Coin unlocks, episode paywalls and any other
pay-per-item surface are deliberately out of scope: they are a separate
decision loop with their own label and their own experiment. Holding one
surface is what keeps the label, the intervention and the measurement
clean enough to be worth arguing about.
