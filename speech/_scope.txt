SPEECH AND AUDIO
================

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
ASR and transcription, speaker diarisation, audio content understanding,
chaptering and highlight detection, TTS and voice cloning, music and
speech tagging, audio quality and moderation.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
Long, unstructured, expensive-to-label inputs. A single hour of audio can
cost more to annotate than a thousand rows of tabular data, so label
efficiency and pretrained transfer are the core of the design.

  - segmentation before modelling: an hour of audio is not one example
  - WER and its failure to reflect what users actually notice
  - code-mixed and low-resource languages with almost no labelled data
  - pretrained audio encoders and when fine-tuning is worth it
  - batch versus streaming inference, and the very different budgets
  - GPU cost per hour processed as the binding constraint
  - metadata generated from audio feeding the recommendation system, so
    errors propagate across folder boundaries

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Auto-chaptering long-form audio episodes.
  02  Transcription pipeline for a code-mixed multilingual catalog.
  03  Audio quality scoring at ingest to gate publication.
