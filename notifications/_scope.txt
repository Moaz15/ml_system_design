NOTIFICATIONS AND MESSAGING
===========================

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Push, email and in-app messaging: who to send to, what to send, when to
send, how often, and when to stay silent.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
The decision is send or do not send, so the right target is INCREMENTAL
effect, not predicted click. A user who would have opened the app anyway
generates a click that the model takes credit for and learns from. This is
the cleanest uplift-modelling domain in the interview set.

  - propensity vs uplift: p(open | send) is the wrong objective
  - the long-term cost that never shows up in the open rate: fatigue,
    notification disable, uninstall - a permanent loss driven by a
    short-term metric
  - send-time optimisation and per-user frequency caps as constraints
  - global holdout as the only honest measurement of the whole channel
  - heavy-tailed users and cannibalisation across campaigns

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Daily push for a content app: who gets it, when, and what.
  02  Optimising for 30-day retention rather than open rate.
  03  Frequency capping and a fatigue model with no direct fatigue label.
