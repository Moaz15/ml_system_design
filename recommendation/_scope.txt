RECOMMENDATION
==============

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Any surface where the system picks items for a user with no query typed.
Homepage rails, "because you listened to", related items, feed ranking,
next-episode autoplay, email digests of content.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
Implicit feedback. There is no ground truth, only behaviour that you chose
to call a label, and the model's own output becomes tomorrow's training
data. Everything hard here follows from those two facts:

  - what counts as a positive (click / dwell / completion / return)
  - retrieval then ranking, because the catalog does not fit the ranker
  - position bias and the popularity feedback loop
  - cold start for new users, new items and new markets - three problems
  - diversity, freshness and the exploration/exploitation trade-off

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Homepage rails for an audio app with 18 months of listen logs.
      EASY. Abundant data, clean label. Drills the spine end to end.
  02  The same surface on day zero - new market, no listen history.
      Cold start from nothing. Data strategy, not architecture.
  03  Related-content rail: item-to-item at catalog scale.
  04  Feed ranking with multiple competing objectives and a value model.
  05  Recommendations for a two-sided marketplace where creators churn if
      their reach collapses.
