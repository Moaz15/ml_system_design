ADS
===

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
CTR and CVR prediction, auction and ranking, bidding, budget pacing,
audience targeting, frequency capping, incrementality measurement.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
The only domain where the model's output is spent directly as money, so
CALIBRATION matters more than ranking quality. A well-ranked but badly
calibrated model bids wrong and loses cash on every impression.

  - expected value = p(click) x p(convert | click) x value, so each term
    must be a probability, not a score
  - delayed and partial conversion labels, and attribution windows
  - extreme sparsity and high-cardinality categorical features
  - the auction: what the model decides vs what the mechanism decides
  - three-sided objective: user, advertiser, platform
  - incrementality vs correlation - would they have converted anyway

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  CTR prediction for a new in-app ad slot.
  02  Delayed conversions: training when the label lands 7 days later.
  03  Budget pacing and under-delivery.
  04  Measuring incremental lift when you cannot hold out easily.
