MARKETPLACE AND MATCHING
========================

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Two-sided systems where supply and demand must be matched: ride dispatch,
delivery assignment, ETA prediction, surge and dynamic pricing, creator
payouts, seller ranking, inventory allocation.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
Users compete for the same finite supply, so treatment affects control.
The standard user-randomised A/B is BIASED here, and knowing that is the
single strongest signal in this domain.

  - interference and network effects; switchback and region-level tests
  - optimising for the match, not for either side's local metric
  - prediction feeding an optimiser: the model is one input to an
    assignment problem, not the decision itself
  - ETA as a regression with an asymmetric cost - early and late are not
    equally bad, and the prediction changes the outcome it predicts
  - supply-side churn as the slow, expensive, hard-to-reverse damage
  - liquidity and cold start in a new city or category

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  ETA prediction, and why the obvious loss function is wrong.
  02  Dispatch and matching under supply scarcity.
  03  Launching in a new city with no supply and no demand history.
  04  Creator payout ranking without collapsing the long tail.
