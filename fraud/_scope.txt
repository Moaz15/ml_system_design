FRAUD AND ABUSE
===============

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Payment fraud, account takeover, fake accounts and bots, promo and refund
abuse, spam, listing scams, engagement farming.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
An adversary that reacts to your model. The data distribution is not
drifting by accident - it is being moved by someone who profits from your
false negatives. This changes retraining from hygiene into a security
property.

  - extreme class imbalance, often under 0.1% positives
  - labels arrive late (chargebacks at 60-90 days) and are incomplete:
    you never learn about the fraud you blocked, only what you let through
  - asymmetric costs - a blocked good customer is not a free mistake
  - threshold setting as an economic decision, not an F1 optimisation
  - rules engine plus model, and why the rules never go away
  - graph and velocity features; adversaries evade features, not models
  - feedback loop: the blocked population is censored from training data

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Payment fraud detection for a subscription product.
  02  Fake account and bot detection at signup, with near-zero friction
      tolerance for real users.
  03  Promo abuse on a new referral campaign, with no historical labels.
  04  Engagement farming on creator payouts.
