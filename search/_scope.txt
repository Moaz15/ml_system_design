SEARCH
======

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Anything where the user states intent in a query. Product search, content
search, autocomplete and query suggestion, query understanding, semantic
and hybrid retrieval, "no results" recovery.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
Intent is declared, so relevance is judgeable - which means human
judgements, graded labels and offline eval are actually available here,
unlike in recommendation. The tension is between what a rater calls
relevant and what a user buys or finishes.

  - query understanding: intent classes, spell correction, entities
  - navigational vs broad vs long-tail queries fail differently
  - lexical (BM25) vs vector vs hybrid retrieval, and why hybrid wins
  - relevance judgements: guidelines, rater agreement, cost per judgement
  - NDCG on click labels greenlighting a model that loses money

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Search ranking for a content catalog: retrieval, ranking, eval.
  02  Zero-result and low-recall queries on a small or new catalog.
  03  Autocomplete under a 50ms budget.
  04  Semantic search retrofit: is a vector index worth it over BM25.
  05  Multilingual and code-mixed queries with no labelled data.
