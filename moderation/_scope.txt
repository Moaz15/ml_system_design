CONTENT MODERATION AND TRUST
============================

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Policy enforcement on user or creator content: harmful content detection,
NSFW, spam, copyright, age-rating, review authenticity, appeals routing.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
The label is a POLICY, not a behaviour. It has to be written by humans
before it can be learned, and reasonable annotators will disagree. This is
the folder where human-in-the-loop design is the system design.

  - policy -> annotation guideline -> label, and measuring rater agreement
  - precision vs recall as a values decision with real-world harm on both
    sides, not a tuning knob
  - the review queue IS the architecture: auto-remove / auto-allow /
    escalate, and how the thresholds carve those three zones
  - capacity planning against a finite human review team
  - appeals as a label-correction channel and a fairness audit
  - multimodal: text, audio, image, plus context and intent
  - rare, high-severity classes where a single miss is the whole story

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Moderating user-uploaded audio at ingest, with a 40-person review
      team and a 5-minute publish SLA.
  02  A new policy category with zero labelled examples.
  03  Appeals routing and measuring wrongful-removal rate.
