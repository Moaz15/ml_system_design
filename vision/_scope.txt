VISION
======

WHAT BELONGS HERE
-----------------
Image and video understanding: classification, detection, OCR, visual
search, thumbnail selection, video segmentation, on-device inference.

THE SKILL THIS FOLDER DRILLS
----------------------------
Annotation is the budget. Bounding boxes and segmentation masks cost real
money per image, so the design is largely about spending that budget well:
active learning, weak labels, synthetic data, pretrained backbones.

  - label cost per example drives the whole data strategy
  - active learning: label the examples the model is least sure about
  - augmentation and synthetic data, and where they stop helping
  - edge and on-device constraints: quantisation, distillation, model size
  - video is not images - temporal redundancy, sampling, and cost
  - distribution shift from cameras, lighting, geography, demographics
  - fairness auditing by segment where the harm is visible and public

CANDIDATE CASES
---------------
  01  Thumbnail selection to maximise click-through without clickbait.
  02  Visual search over a product catalog.
  03  On-device classification under a 30ms and 10MB budget.
